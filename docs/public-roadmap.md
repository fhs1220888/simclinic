# Roadmap

The diagnostic CLI, evidence-linked checks, fixed OpenFOAM integration and
bounded missing-output recovery are implemented. Specific controlled studies
are recorded in [validation scope](validation-scope.md).

Next development priorities:

1. Publish a reviewed, portable evidence distribution with hashes and exact
   prerequisites for archive-dependent replay and candidate delivery checks.
2. Improve adapters and developer-facing diagnostic/retest workflows using
   concrete records, while preserving four-state evidence semantics.
3. Define fresh tasks and questions before further model/CFD evaluation. Used
   C0–C10 and previous study tasks cannot establish new independent benefit.
4. Measure naturally occurring eligible failures before claiming natural
   recovery benefit. Continue to report total costs and failed attempts.

Web UI, MCP, other simulation domains, HPC and general automatic repair are
future scope. No date or comprehensive success-rate promise is set.
